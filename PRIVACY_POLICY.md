# Privacy Policy for CourseHub

**Last Updated: September 14, 2026**

CourseHub ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal data and your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our CourseHub mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Information**: When you register or log in, we collect your display name, username/user ID, email address, and authentication credentials securely stored via local database and cloud authentication services.
- **Payment Verification Information**: When purchasing or enrolling in a course, you may provide your sender transaction ID, phone number, and an optional payment receipt screenshot. We use Android's zero-permission Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`) to access only the specific image you pick, without requesting general storage access.
- **Course Progress & Activity**: Course enrollments, lesson progress, quiz attempts, and completion statuses are saved to provide a seamless learning experience across your devices.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

- **To Provide and Maintain Services**: To grant access to purchased courses, display lessons, videos, notes, and verify enrollment status.
- **For Payment Verification**: Admin administrators review submitted transaction IDs and receipt screenshots to approve course enrollments.
- **To Send Critical Notifications**: If enabled, to notify you when your course enrollment has been approved or rejected.
- **Customer Support**: To assist you via WhatsApp support or email when you request help.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored locally using an encrypted Room database and securely synced with Google Firebase Firestore cloud infrastructure using industry-standard TLS encryption.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information or payment screenshots to any third parties or advertisers.

---

### 4. Zero Storage Permissions

CourseHub strictly adheres to Google Play developer policies. We do **not** ask for `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` permissions. We only receive access to the single payment receipt image you explicitly select via the Android system Photo Picker.

---

### 5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated enrollment data at any time. You can clear your data directly within the app under Account Settings or contact our support team.
