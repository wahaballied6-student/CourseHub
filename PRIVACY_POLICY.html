Privacy Policy for CourseHub

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

CourseHub ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the CourseHub mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Your name, username, email address, and authentication credentials for logging in and keeping your course progress synchronized.
  • Course Enrollments & Receipts: When you enroll in a course, your transaction ID, contact phone, and optional payment receipt image selected via Android's Photo Picker are collected solely for manual enrollment approval.
  • Learning Progress: Completed lessons, active course lists, and reading achievements.

2. Data Permissions & Media Security

CourseHub uses the system Photo Picker (zero broad storage permissions). We do not read your device gallery, files, or external storage beyond the specific receipt you choose to attach.

3. Cloud Synchronization & Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase Firestore to securely synchronize course catalogs and enrollment statuses in real time. We do not sell or monetize personal user data.

4. Account Deletion & Rights

You can request data deletion or account removal anytime via the in-app Account Settings or by reaching out to our team below.
